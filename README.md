# ✈️ Travel Planner

Travel Planner is a smart travel-planning app that creates personalized itineraries based on your destination, trip duration, travel preferences, and real-time weather.

## Features

- 📍 **Destination Selection**  
  Choose the city you want to visit.

- 📅 **Trip Duration**  
  Set the number of days for your trip.

- 🎒 **Travel Personas**  
  Choose between:
  - Backpacker
  - Luxury
  - Family

- 🌦️ **Real-Time Weather**  
  Fetches current weather information for the destination.

- 🗓️ **Weather-Aware Itineraries**  
  Automatically adapts activities based on weather conditions, such as suggesting indoor activities during rain and outdoor activities during suitable weather.

- 🎯 **Personalized Recommendations**  
  Activities and plans are tailored according to the selected travel persona.

- 🗺️ **Location & Maps**  
  View destinations and itinerary locations on a map.

- 📏 **Distance & Travel Time**  
  Calculate distances and estimated travel times between itinerary stops.

- 💾 **Saved Trips**  
  Save generated trips and access them later.

- 🔗 **Shareable Itineraries**  
  Share your travel plan with others.

- 🤖 **AI Enhancement**  
  AI can enhance itinerary descriptions and recommendations while the core itinerary generation remains functional without AI.

  ## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Swift & SwiftUI** — iOS app development and UI
- **SwiftData** — Local trip persistence
- **MapKit & CoreLocation** — Maps, locations, and distance calculations
- **URLSession & Codable** — Weather API and JSON networking
- **AI API** — Personalized itinerary enhancement
- **ShareLink** — Sharing generated itineraries
